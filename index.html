    Quick Help

    Step

    • Click cells to toggle hits. Press Space to play or stop; adjust tempo and swing while playing.
    • Use + to load a row sample, the arrow to change its instrument, and Add instrument to add a row. MIX controls row pitch, level, swing, and EQ.
    • Hold Shift and click cells to select them; press Delete to clear the selection. Use Browser → Import drum sounds to add samples as rows.

    Piano Roll

    • Choose an instrument, click to add notes, and drag notes or their edges to edit them. Click the bar ruler to move the playhead. Drum kits are available here, not in Step.
    • Arrow keys move selected notes by a snap step or semitone. Shift + ←/→ changes note length; Ctrl + ↑/↓ moves notes by an octave.
    • Press P to place the current non-empty Mel or pattern at the Track View playhead.

    Arrangement / Track View

    • Add an instrument track for an empty Mel lane, choose its instrument from the track label, then drop or paste Mels onto it. Import recordings or add audio tracks to arrange audio. Click the bar ruler to move the playhead; playback combines tracks at the project tempo.
    • Use each track's mute button, S button, and volume slider to mute, solo, or set its level.
    • Use Snap for beat-grid placement, movement, and resizing. Drag the upper-right handle to repeat; the lower-right handle trims or extends (extensions are silent).
    • Press P in Step or Piano Roll to place content; press S to split clips at the playhead. Arrow keys nudge selected clips by one bar.

    DJ

    • Choose a source for Deck A or B, then use Play, Sync, and the nudge buttons to mix.
    • Drag a deck waveform to stage its entry; drag the arrangement ruler to seek. Space pauses or resumes. In full DJ View, M toggles master mute; [ + M toggles Deck A mute, and ] + M toggles Deck B mute. L loops the active deck's current bar; use [ + L or ] + L for Deck A or B. Press = to add a bar, - to remove one, and L again to clear the loop.

    Mixer

    • Open Mixer or choose EQ beside a track to adjust level, three-band EQ, and effects.

    Projects & shortcuts

    • Projects save in this browser. Use the project menu to save, import, rename, create, or switch projects. Choose Collaborate to create or join a shared room; enable audio in the room to follow synchronized playback. Export a song as WAV, MP3, or a portable BPM project; export patterns and Mels from their editors.
    • Use ⌘ instead of Ctrl on Mac. In Track View, Ctrl + C/V copies or pastes selected clips; select a track without selecting clips to copy the whole track. Paste onto a selected compatible track, or use Ctrl + Shift + V to paste onto a new track of the same type. Mel clips pasted onto an instrument track play with that track's instrument. Ctrl + Shift + C copies the selected track even when clips are selected. Piano Roll notes also support Ctrl + C/V; Ctrl + X cuts selected notes or clips; Delete/Backspace removes selected notes or clips.
    • Ctrl + Z undoes the last action in the active editor.
    • In Piano Roll or Track View, hold Alt/Option and drag a marquee to deselect items inside it; hold Shift while dragging to add to the selection.
