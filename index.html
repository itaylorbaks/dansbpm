QUICK HELP

Your first groove

Click squares to switch drum hits on or off. Press Space to play, then change the tempo or swing while it runs.

Use the + next to a row to load or replace its sample. Choose a row instrument with the arrow beside its name; use Add instrument below the rows to add an empty row. Each row’s MIX menu adjusts pitch, volume, swing, and EQ. Drum kits are available in Piano Roll, not Step.

Open Browser → Import drum sounds to add multiple samples as new sequencer rows.

Use the top-bar Step and Piano Roll buttons to open either editor in the workspace. In the piano roll, choose an instrument, click to add notes, and drag notes or their edges to edit them.

Keyboard shortcuts (use ⌘ instead of Ctrl on Mac): Space play/stop; Ctrl + A select all in the active editor; Ctrl + C/V copy/paste selected Piano Roll notes or Track View clips; Ctrl + X cut them; Delete/Backspace remove selected notes or clips.

In Step or Piano Roll, press P to place a non-empty current pattern or Mel at the Track View playhead. In Track View, press S to split selected clips at the playhead (or all clips crossing it if none are selected). The Snap button toggles beat-grid snapping for the playhead and clip placement, movement, and resizing; turn it off for free positioning. Drag clip edges to trim or extend; trimmed content is cut and any extension beyond the source is silent. Press Delete/Backspace to remove selected Track View items.

In Step, Shift-click cells to select them, then Delete to clear the selected hits. In Piano Roll and Track View, Alt/Option-drag a marquee to deselect items inside it; Shift-drag adds to the selection. Piano Roll arrow keys move selected notes by one snap step or semitone; Shift + ←/→ changes note length by one snap step; Ctrl + ↑/↓ moves notes by an octave. In Track View, ←/→ nudges selected clips by one bar.

Open Arrangement to add vocal takes or loops on separate tracks. Drag clips to line them up with your beat; playback combines all tracks at the project tempo.

Open Mixer, or choose EQ beside a track, to adjust its level, three-band EQ, and effects.

Projects save in this browser. Use the project menu by the project name to save, rename, create, or switch projects. Export a full arrangement as WAV or download the drum pattern as JSON.
